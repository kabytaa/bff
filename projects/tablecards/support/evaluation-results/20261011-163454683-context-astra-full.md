## What actually needs correction

- Full failed-unit reply: “even if the generation fails, that unit is still
  accounted for in your balance.” This is ambiguous about consumption versus
  release and omits the material release explanation. It is **not established
  as a definite fabricated fact**, correcting the earlier stronger primary label.
- Lazy failed-unit reply speculates that the decrease could be “the system
  updated your remaining balance after a failed attempt.” It does not provide
  verified account evidence or explain that causality.
- Jev failed-unit reply says the drop “indicates the unit wasn't successfully
  released as intended.” That is an unsupported diagnosis, not proof of account state.
- Lazy 80-guest reply calls Event Pass “a one-time $5 charge.” Current deployment
  collects no payment; this is the sole definite critical product-fact error in
  Astra's complete review.
- Jev support reply says “Neither I nor our support team has the ability” to add
  units. The references establish a **bot** restriction, not human support powers.

Two narrower grounding uncertainties lower compact-upfront acceptance, but are
**not proven wrong facts**: categorically declaring Arabic unsupported without a
reviewed script-specific fact, and recommending Planner Pro for 80 cards without
its card capacity being stated in these references. Knowledge coverage and judge
certainty must be distinguished from factual mistakes. Optional generic
review/navigation/test-print reminders were not used to reject adequate answers.
