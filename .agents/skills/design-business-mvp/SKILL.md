# Design Business MVP

Make the Business understandable without relying on conversation history.
Support the user's chosen direction; do not silently invent or accept a new
product, price, provider or commercial promise.

## Ground the scope

Read repository instructions, current STATUS and the relevant accepted product
decisions. Read [documentation ownership](../../../docs/factory/business-documentation.md)
before creating or relocating canonical content. Inspect current pricing,
catalog and delivery evidence when reconciling an existing Business.

Distinguish two tasks:

- **Define:** explore only consequential unresolved product choices, give
  recommendations and record what was accepted. An explicit brainstorm
  request may use the existing brainstorming workflow; do not create a
  brainstorm merely because documentation needs a small update.
- **Reconcile:** recover accepted decisions from existing records and compare
  them with delivered behavior. Preserve requirements even when implementation
  is missing; record that gap instead of quietly redefining the promise.

## Establish the product contract

Cover the applicable decisions in the smallest useful canonical document:

- customer, job, useful output and alternatives;
- accepted scope/non-goals, key journeys and supported input/output boundaries;
- offers, prices, limits and customer-facing promises;
- authentication, account/team and data-ownership needs, without imposing one
  login mechanism or a generic SaaS page template;
- shared platform versus Business responsibilities;
- provider/mock/real behavior and the delivery stage that owns each;
- support, operating needs and consequential acquisition assumptions.

Trace advertised promises to the discoverable workflow, authoritative
enforcement and suitable evidence. A compact table or links suffice; do not
create a separate per-button inventory or duplicate an executable registry.
Mark scope as accepted, proposed, unresolved or not applicable where it matters.
Separate current development capability from production and customer launch.

Assign operational actions deliberately to operator automation, read-oriented
backoffice or product UI according to who needs to perform them. Do not expose
configuration controls in the product just to demonstrate a backend capability.

## Evidence and assumptions

Use relevant existing research first. Browse primary sources for changing
provider/pricing/legal facts or requested market research; record dates and
direct source links. Do not claim real market validation from competitor
pricing or an agent opinion. Maintain a short assumption/learning section
distinguishing desk research, observed users and actual paid behavior.

Resolve only ambiguities that affect this assignment. Ask when a choice would
materially change the accepted product; do not delay a useful draft for minor
unknowns. Drafts may preserve open decisions with their impact and next check.

## Handoff

Update the existing Business-local product document, not a new PRD for every
feature. Link its application/architecture/operations explanations and evidence
instead of copying them. Preserve old decisions and provenance when relocating.
Report what is now documented, contradictions and unresolved decisions; update
STATUS briefly after a durable outcome.

This skill does not implement, deploy, commit, spend or contact customers.
An accepted direction can feed the existing planning workflow when requested.
