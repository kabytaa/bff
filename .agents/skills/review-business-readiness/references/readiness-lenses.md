# Applicable readiness lenses

Use these as questions against the accepted Business and review boundary, not
as a universal feature or technology list. Record a reason when a lens does
not apply. Verify changing provider or jurisdiction-specific facts through
current primary sources rather than asserting them from memory.

## Product and commercial truth

Are promises, supported inputs/outputs, limits, prices and mock/real behavior
truthful and reachable? Are purchases, renewals, cancellation/refunds and
restricted-account remediation evidenced when this stage includes them?
Separate willingness-to-pay/acquisition hypotheses from observed customers.

## Experience and accessibility

Does current app-review evidence cover the in-scope desktop/mobile journeys,
states, roles and changed dependencies? Inspect focus/keyboard, labels, errors,
contrast/reflow and assistive-technology evidence proportionately. Automation
alone is not proof of conformance or understandable visual hierarchy.

## Security and privacy

Check identity/session and account isolation, authoritative authorization,
secrets and diagnostics, uploads/file access, idempotency/concurrency,
provider callbacks and failure recovery. Inventory data, who receives it,
retention/deletion and abuse/spend exposure. Negative tests must demonstrate
relevant denials, not merely that a protected route exists. Do not publish real
customer data or credentials as review evidence.

## Legal and disclosures

Check business/contact identity, applicable privacy/terms/cookie consent,
digital delivery, price/tax/renewal/cancellation/refund disclosures,
marketing consent and uploaded/generated content rights. Match checks to the
actual seller/market/data profile. Record missing evidence and genuinely
unresolved legal questions; use qualified advice when required rather than
inventing a legal sign-off. A development simulation must clearly say no charge.

## Performance and compatibility

Verify supported engines and dimensions, bundle/loading boundaries, realistic
task/input size, render/job latency, provider limits and error states against
actual budgets. Do not invent a universal performance number. Distinguish
emulated mobile/WebKit from physical device evidence.

## Operations and deployment

For the requested stage, check exact targets/versions, environment guardrails,
health and product smoke, actionable monitoring/diagnostics, support, recovery
and rollback. Confirm recurring lifecycle work has an owner/mechanism rather
than requiring an undocumented manual action. Production and customer launch
need their own evidence; a dev pass cannot be promoted by changing its label.
